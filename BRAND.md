# Brand notice

The code in this repository is released under the MIT licence in [LICENSE](LICENSE). You are welcome to reuse it and to borrow ideas from it.

The University of Bristol and Bristol Flight Lab brand is not licensed for reuse. The MIT licence does not cover it.

## What the brand covers

- The University of Bristol name, crest and logo.
- The Bristol Flight Lab name, bird-and-aircraft mark and wordmark.
- The colour palette, including Bristol red `#B01C2E` and the six accent colours.
- The visual design of the slide layouts, including the red bands and rule, the slanted edge, and the placement of the marks.

In this repository that means:

- the SVG files in `assets/`;
- the copies of those files embedded in `themes/flightlab.css`;
- the colour and layout values in `src/flightlab.css`, where they reproduce the design.

## Using the theme

The theme is for University of Bristol and Bristol Flight Lab teaching materials. Slides made with it should follow the University's brand guidelines.

## Reusing the code elsewhere

If you build on this code for anything else, remove `assets/` and the images embedded in `themes/flightlab.css`. Change the colours and restyle the layouts, so that your slides cannot be mistaken for University of Bristol or Bristol Flight Lab material.
